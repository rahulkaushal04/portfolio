About Me

From pipelines to platform

Backend and data work since 2022, most of it on the platform layer other teams deploy on.

I joined Honeywell in 2022 on the data side, writing PySpark pipelines on Databricks that fed field service analytics and an internal maintenance chatbot. In 2024 I moved to Forge Platform, the team that builds the layer those pipelines feed. That work became ForgeML: training orchestration, a model registry, security scanning, and deployment out to Databricks Serving, AKS and edge devices.

The part I care about sits where the infrastructure meets the people using it. Getting deployment down from three days to thirty minutes mattered more to the fifteen data scientists on the other side of the API than any single service I wrote to get there.

Open source works the same way for me. When I hit a bug in a library I depend on, reading the source and sending a patch is usually quicker than building a workaround I will have to remember later. That is where the fixes to facebookresearch/exca and aio-libs/async-lru came from, and it is why I still maintain DepKeeper.

Currently working through Generative AI System Design Interview by Ali Aminian and Hao Sheng.
