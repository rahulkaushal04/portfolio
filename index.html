About Me

Root cause, not workaround

Two things that show up in most of what I build.

One is caring where the infrastructure meets the people relying on it: a data scientist who shouldn't have to care if a model is serving from Databricks, AKS or an edge device, or an operator whose only signal that something is wrong is an appliance that stopped responding.

The other is reading the source instead of working around it. When I hit a bug in a library I depend on, sending a patch is usually quicker than a fix I'd only have to remember later. That's where the patches to facebookresearch/exca and aio-libs/async-lru came from, and why I still maintain DepKeeper.

Currently working through Generative AI System Design Interview by Ali Aminian and Hao Sheng, and otherwise deep in Elden Ring and Hollow Knight.

Patent

Granted

Filed from the multi-tenant platform work at Honeywell.

US 12,717,808 Issued Aug 25, 2026

Data management in a multi-tenant environment

Filed while working on Honeywell's multi-tenant cloud platform. The problem was keeping each customer's data fully separate when everyone shares the same backend, without spinning up a separate database per customer.

The patent covers doing that at the data level: tagging and filtering records so they can be split and routed to the right tenant by customer, product or a custom rule, instead of relying on separate schemas or databases for isolation.

  • Multi-tenancy
  • Data isolation
  • Cloud platform
